# 12-week plan (locked 21 Nov 2026, rev 3)

Week 1 starts Mon 23 Nov 2026. Week 12 ends Sun 14 Feb 2027.
Month gate Fri 11 Dec (path only, off a regular session). Checkpoints Mon 21 Dec, Mon 18 Jan, Mon 15 Feb.

Checkpoint dates moved from the Sundays originally proposed to the following Mondays, because the
protocol requires the core blocks to be hit fresh at the start of a Monday bay session.

## Week

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Bay, 45-60 min |
| Tue | Workout A: heavy legs and core, ~45 min |
| Wed | Workout B: upper body and rotation, no heavy legs, ~45 min |
| Thu | Bay, 45-60 min |
| Fri | Workout C: moderate full body, lower loads 70% or less of Tuesday, ~45 min |
| Sat | Short bay, one block, 30-40 min |
| Sun | 18 holes in the bay plus a 45-60 min brisk walk |

Daily: three walks, plus 10-15 min GolfForever in the morning. On workout days it replaces the warm-up.
Bay goes first if a bay day and a weight day ever share a day.

## Morning slot

GolfForever on the 30-day free trial, capped at the first 10-15 minutes of its daily plan. Its own plan
runs 30-45 minutes, which would break one-session-a-day.

- Bands and a homemade bar are in use from week 1.
- No weighted-ball or overload swinging until the coach has seen it. The weighted ball is a
  swing-weighted warm-up club about 2.5x a driver's feel.
- No heavy lower-body work from the app on Tuesday mornings.
- Its swing language never comes into the bay.
- Mobility, rotation and band activation only. Not its strength plan.
- Fallback decided at week 4: if it keeps prescribing strength that clashes with Tuesday and Friday,
  switch to Fit For Golf.

## Weights

- Week 1 is the load-finding week. No 1RM test: a max lower-body effort two days before a bay day
  defeats the Tuesday-only rule, and a separate pre-start session breaks one-session-a-day.
- Per lift, work up until 8-10 reps leaves 3 in reserve. That is the anchor. Log it.
- Weeks 1-4: technique and full range, 3 RIR, loads at the anchor.
- Weeks 5-8: add about 5% or 1-2 reps when all sets are clean, 2 RIR. Flex days 25 Dec and 1 Jan.
- Weeks 9-12: lighter, fast intent, rotational power, 2 RIR.
- Strength metric at each checkpoint: the 8-10RM load per lift. No estimated 1RM anywhere.
- Heavy lower body Tuesday only. Sunday stays low-load.

### Equipment
- Adjustable dumbbells, a pair to about 50 lb each, needed from week 1. Bands cannot progressively
  load a squat or hinge through a foundation phase.
- Med ball 8-10 lb before week 9, for rotational throws into a wall. The release is the one transfer
  element bands cannot reproduce.
- Bands and the homemade bar cover the morning slot and the weeks 9-12 rotational pulls.

## Bay

- Template: 10 min warm-up, 10-15 driver, 10-15 7-iron.
- Stop a block if its smash drops 0.05 below the first block, or if the last 5 shots are more than
  1.8 deg more out-to-in than the first 5.
- Driver weight stays Neutral. If it changes, the baseline restarts.
- Cue order: cup less (weeks 1-2), arm hang at address (weeks 3-4), coach to set from week 5.
- Weight-shift and sequencing drills start week 1, in their own block AFTER the counted blocks:
  short club, partial speed, 8-10 reps, logged separately. During counted blocks the only swing
  thought is the current cue. The drill never becomes the bay cue.
- Fit the irons first (7i path -4.0 or better twice with centered offset), the driver at 75% within 3 deg.

## Checkpoints

Same conditions every time: 10 uncounted balls, then the core, then the survey. Start of a Monday bay
session, same aim spot, filmed face-on and down-the-line. About 60 counted shots.

**Core, fixed, hit fresh and first:** driver 15, 7-iron 15. These never rotate.
Metrics: median path, face, face-to-path, face SD, % within 3 deg, median offset, heel hits at 1.0 in
or more, impact height, low point, smash, attack angle, spin, launch, carry, first 5 vs last 5.

**Survey, rotating, after the core:** 50 deg, 9-iron, 5-iron, 5-wood. One club promoted to 15 shots per
checkpoint, the rest at 5. Week 4 the 5-iron, week 8 the 50 deg, week 12 the 5-wood.
At 5 shots: medians only. At 15: the full metric list.

Weeks 8 and 12 also get a random-order 3-3-3 check and a fitting rehearsal of 7-8 clubs at 10-15 shots.

Low point is now tracked at every checkpoint. It is in the export and was previously thought not to be.

## TPI

The coach is not TPI certified and there is no consumer route to a real screen. The plan runs without
TPI measurements. If travel puts him near a certified pro, a one-time screen is worth doing, but nothing
here depends on it. At week 4, if the weight-shift work has not moved the 7-iron low-on-face strikes or
the late-set path drift, that goes back to the coach with video and data.

## Calories

About 700-900 active on session days, about 500 on lighter days. Saturday is the lightest at roughly 600,
so add a walk or hike. Judge by the weekly weight trend, not a daily number.

## Open items

selection. App prices unverified.
